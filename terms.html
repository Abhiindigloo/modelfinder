What it does

Model Finder is advisory. It audits the models a repository uses, drafts and runs evaluations with your own keys, ranks results, generates routing configurations and estimates cost and memory. It never changes your active model, deploys anything or starts a service. The MCP server writes no files and makes network connections only when you explicitly run the opt-in modelfinder_run_eval tool.

Outputs are estimates

  • Prices, model IDs and availability can be wrong or out of date. Verify them on each provider's pages.
  • Eval results reflect only your examples and checks. A small sample is a smoke test, not proof of quality.
  • Figures on this website, including the cost-ranking plate and the film, are illustrative.
  • Review and test generated routing and LiteLLM configs before use.
